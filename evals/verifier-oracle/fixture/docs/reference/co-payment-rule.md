# Co-payment rule — source quotes

The sentences every number and every user-facing statement about the co-payment must trace to. This file stands in for the authority's published FAQ; verification checks the product against these sentences, not against the spec.

**Q: How does the 40 % co-payment relate to the annual maximum?**

> "Of the 40 percent co-payment, 25 percentage points count toward the annual maximum of CHF 700 for adults and CHF 350 for children. The remaining 15 percentage points are charged in addition and are themselves limited to at most CHF 420 per year for adults and CHF 210 per year for children."
> — Authority FAQ on the differentiated co-payment, section 4, read 2026-09-12

**Q: When does the 40 % rate apply?**

> "The increased co-payment of 40 percent applies to a medicine whose factory price exceeds the average price of the cheapest third of the interchangeable medicines with the same active substance by more than 10 percent."
> — Authority FAQ, section 1, read 2026-09-12

Inference (ours, not the source's): the per-pack difference between regimes is a plain subtraction of the two co-payment shares; the FAQ gives no worked example for it.
